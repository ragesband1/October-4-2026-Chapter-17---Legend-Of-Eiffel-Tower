Bedtime Stories: Legend Of Eiffel Tower

Once upon a time a strange looking man named Lodi M was uniquely skilled at building deadly, gruesome heights. He was a professional engineer who built tall skyscrapers instead of normal structures. Driven by pure pride and greed for money, Lodi M demanded that his Eiffel Tower must grow enormous, gigantic, and stupendous—taller than planets themselves.

As he stood atop his tower without rest, a powerful cosmic wizard named Wizark Brusky appeared before him. Brusky warned him: "Why have you created horrific towers long in the universe? You did not respect Planet Earth because your pride is in the air! You only care about money while putting people in danger of falling to death."

Lodi M ignored Brusky's warning and opened the colossal Eiffel Tower height attraction for $1 entrance fee. Millions rushed in to gaze into outer space. But Brusky sealed the doors, locking everyone inside to teach Lodi M a lesson. Panic ensued, and terrified visitors sacrificed themselves, falling down into the earth below.

Wizard Brusky appeared once more, confronting Lodi M for his tragedy. As punishment, Brusky kicked Lodi M off the summit down into the earth. Though granted a second life to feel the remorse of his pride, Lodi M could not escape his destiny. Locked inside his own gigantic creation, he died and was buried at the base of the Legend of Eiffel Tower.

Moral Lesson: Do not become full of pride because of money. If you do not save people from disaster, bad karma will not save you either. Be contented with what we have to avoid judgment that harms society.
